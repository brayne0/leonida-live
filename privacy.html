Privacy Policy – Leonida Live Clipper

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Leonida Live Clipper ("the App") is a private tool operated by Brayne Duarte for the YouTube channel Leonida Live. It is used only by its operator and is not offered to the public. Contact: brayneduarte13@gmail.com.

2. What the App does

The App creates short highlight videos from public live streams of streamers who have given permission to be clipped, and uploads those videos to the Leonida Live YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

3. Data we access through YouTube API Services

  • OAuth access to the Leonida Live channel only (scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly), granted by the channel owner.
  • Basic information about the Leonida Live channel and the videos it uploads (IDs, titles, privacy status), used to confirm uploads.

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, viewer or channel.

4. How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the operator's own computer or server and is never shared or sold. Upload logs (video ID, date, file name) are kept locally to avoid duplicate uploads.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

6. Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

7. Deletion

Revoking access and deleting the local token removes all data the App holds. Requests: brayneduarte13@gmail.com.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
