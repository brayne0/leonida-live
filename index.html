About

Leonida Live is a YouTube channel that publishes short highlight clips (YouTube Shorts) of GTA 6 gameplay from live streamers who have given us permission to clip their streams. Every video credits the streamer and links to their original channel.

Our internal tool, Leonida Live Clipper, finds highlight moments in streams we are allowed to clip, edits them into vertical videos with captions, and uploads them to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It only uploads to the channel owned by Leonida Live and does not access any other user's account or data.
